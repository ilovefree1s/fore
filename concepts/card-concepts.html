FORE card concepts

Five directions, each shown as one Fore-Mat (Wolf!) and one Power Up (Air Horn!). Pick one, or point at the pieces you like from each.

1. Vintage Scorecard cream paper, green ink, postmark badge

Looks like an old club scorecard. Ruled lines, double border, serif titles, a red rubber-stamp for points. Power Ups go a shade darker with rust ink so you can tell the piles apart at a glance.

2. Country Club navy and gold, chevron band, big condensed type

Bold and preppy. A dark title band with a striped edge, tall condensed lettering, a pennant-shaped points tag. Power Ups swap navy for a deep red band. Reads well from across a cart.

3. Pop Comic halftone dots, thick outlines, starburst points

Loud and fun, matches the exclamation marks on every card. Halftone border, comic lettering with a drop shadow, a starburst for points. Fore-Mats are yellow and orange, Power Ups are electric blue.

4. Night Round dark felt, neon lime, faint grid

Modern and a little arcade. Dark cards with a glowing points tile, outlined format tag, tight grotesk type. Power Ups go violet. Easiest on the eyes in bright sun since the whole phone stays dark.
